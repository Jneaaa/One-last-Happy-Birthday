Happy Birthday

Happy birthday, to the person I once called mine. Although we don't talk to each other anymore, I wish and pray you achieve everything you hope for. Despite our past I want you to know I will keep my promise, I will continue to support you even if not necessary and cheer silently.

I don't really know where we stand now, but I do know one thing, you were once someone very important in my life until this day you are still very important in my life, and that feeling will never disappear just like that.

I hope today you feel appreciated, loved, and at peace, even if that peace doesn't come from me anymore. Thank you for everything, the memories, the lessons, and even the pain, because all of it changed me into who I am today.

I wish you growth, happiness, and a life that treats you kindly. And somewhere in my heart I still hope that one day we can find our way back. I love my Nathaniel...

With so much love,

- Janea
